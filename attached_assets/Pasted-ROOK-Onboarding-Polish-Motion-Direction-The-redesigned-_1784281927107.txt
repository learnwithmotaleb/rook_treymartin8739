ROOK Onboarding Polish & Motion Direction

The redesigned onboarding flow is structurally complete. Your objective is not to redesign the screens or change the copy. Instead, elevate the experience so it feels like a world-class consumer product that users immediately associate with quality, trust, and advanced AI.

Approach this as a senior product designer and motion designer, not as an engineer adding animations.

Overall Goal

Transform the onboarding from a collection of screens into a cohesive, emotionally engaging journey. Every interaction should reinforce the ROOK brand as premium, intelligent, trustworthy, and technologically advanced.

Avoid making the interface feel like a generic cybersecurity app. Instead, aim for the level of polish found in Apple’s first-party apps, Arc Browser, Linear, Cash App, and other industry-leading consumer products.

Motion Philosophy

Motion should have purpose.

Every animation should either:

* reinforce trust,
* communicate intelligence,
* guide attention,
* create anticipation,
* provide feedback,
* or make the product feel more alive.

Avoid decorative animations that exist only because they look interesting.

Favor subtlety over spectacle.

Visual Character

Review every screen and identify opportunities to introduce visual character through thoughtful motion, depth, lighting, transitions, illustration, and interactive feedback.

Nothing should feel static or lifeless.

The experience should feel continuously active without becoming distracting.

Cohesion

Every screen should feel like it belongs to the same product.

Ensure there is a consistent visual language across:

* transitions
* spacing
* motion timing
* iconography
* illustrations
* interaction patterns
* progress indicators
* backgrounds
* component behavior

Avoid introducing different animation styles or visual motifs from one screen to another.

Interaction Quality

Audit every user interaction.

Selections, taps, loading states, progress indicators, navigation, and confirmations should all provide premium feedback.

Interactions should feel intentional and refined rather than default or mechanical.

Emotional Journey

Evaluate the emotional progression throughout onboarding.

Each screen should naturally build upon the previous one and guide the user through a complete narrative rather than presenting isolated information.

Look for opportunities to strengthen:

* curiosity
* personalization
* confidence
* anticipation
* trust
* perceived intelligence
* perceived product quality

without adding unnecessary friction.

Brand Presence

Find tasteful ways to strengthen ROOK’s identity throughout the onboarding.

Rather than relying on logos or repeated branding, create a subtle visual language that makes the experience feel memorable and uniquely ROOK.

The onboarding should leave users with a recognizable impression of the brand.

Polish Pass

Perform a comprehensive polish pass across the entire onboarding experience.

Identify anything that feels:

* generic
* unfinished
* abrupt
* repetitive
* visually flat
* emotionally weak
* inconsistent
* overly utilitarian

and improve it while preserving the overall flow and usability.

Constraints

* Do not change the onboarding structure unless absolutely necessary.
* Do not rewrite the copy unless it improves clarity or conversion.
* Prioritize quality over quantity.
* Maintain excellent performance on iOS.
* Keep accessibility in mind for all motion and interactions.
* Every enhancement should feel deliberate and contribute to a premium user experience.

The final onboarding should feel launch-ready and comparable in polish to the best subscription apps currently available on the App Store.
