# 🧠 REPLIT SYSTEM PROMPT — SIMPLE HIGH-CONVERTING 3-TIER SUBSCRIPTION MODEL

## SYSTEM ROLE: Subscription & Feature Access System

You are building a clean, modern, high-converting subscription system for an AI-powered Social Identity Protection app that detects impersonator accounts and stolen content across social media platforms.

The subscription system must feel:

* simple
* premium
* easy to understand
* psychologically optimized for upgrades

Avoid overwhelming users with too many features.

The platform should have ONLY 3 tiers:

1. Free
2. Pro
3. Premium

The difference between tiers should focus mainly on:

* scan frequency
* monitoring depth
* AI intelligence level
* number of protected accounts
* speed of alerts

NOT dozens of random feature checklists.

---

# 🧠 CORE PRODUCT POSITIONING

The app helps users:

* detect fake accounts
* discover impersonators automatically
* monitor stolen photos/videos
* protect their online identity

The product’s core value is:

> “AI continuously watching the internet for accounts pretending to be you.”

---

# 💰 SUBSCRIPTION STRUCTURE

---

# 🆓 FREE TIER

## Purpose:

User acquisition + viral growth

## Included:

### Protection

* Protect 1 identity
* Connect up to 2 social accounts

### AI Monitoring

* Manual scans only
* 1 scan every 14 days

### Detection

* Basic impersonator detection
* Limited visual matching

### Alerts

* Delayed notifications

### Results

* Limited threat visibility
* Partial results blurred

---

## FREE TIER EXPERIENCE

The free tier should feel useful enough to trust the product, but limited enough that serious users naturally upgrade after finding threats.

When threats are detected:

* partially blur results
* hide detailed evidence
* show locked premium analysis

Example message:

> “We found possible impersonator accounts. Upgrade to unlock full threat intelligence.”

---

# 🚀 PRO TIER

## Purpose:

Main paid consumer plan

## Suggested Price:

$12.99–$19.99/month

---

## Included:

### Protection

* Protect up to 5 identities
* Connect up to 10 accounts

### AI Monitoring

* Automatic weekly scans
* Continuous monitoring

### Detection

* Full photo + video matching
* Cross-platform impersonator detection
* Stolen content detection

### Alerts

* Real-time alerts

### Results

* Full impersonator visibility
* AI confidence scores
* Detailed evidence

---

## PRO EXPERIENCE

This tier should feel like:

> “Your AI identity bodyguard.”

The majority of serious users should land here.

---

# 👑 PREMIUM TIER

## Purpose:

Creators, influencers, professionals, businesses

## Suggested Price:

$39.99–$79.99/month

---

## Included:

Everything in Pro PLUS:

### Advanced AI Intelligence

* Daily deep scans
* Advanced visual fingerprinting
* Coordinated fake account detection

### Monitoring Power

* Unlimited protected identities
* Priority AI processing
* Faster detection speeds

### Intelligence Dashboard

* Threat history timeline
* Risk trend analytics
* Downloadable reports

### Priority Support

* Faster support response times

---

## PREMIUM EXPERIENCE

This tier should feel like:

> “Enterprise-grade identity protection powered by advanced AI.”

Premium users should feel continuously protected without needing manual action.
