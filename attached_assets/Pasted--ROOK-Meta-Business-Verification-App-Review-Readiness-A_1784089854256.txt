# ROOK Meta Business Verification & App Review Readiness Audit

Our LLC (Rook Identity Labs LLC) is now finalized, and we're preparing for Meta Business Verification and Meta App Review.

I want you to act as a senior Meta Platform engineer and perform a complete audit of the entire codebase, backend, frontend, APIs, authentication flow, website, and Meta configuration.

Do NOT make assumptions. Inspect the project and produce a detailed report.

## Business Verification

Verify that everything required for Meta Business Verification is present or identify what is missing.

Check for:

* Business name consistency
* Company name throughout the app
* Website branding
* Privacy Policy
* Terms of Service
* Data Deletion Instructions page
* Contact email
* Contact phone
* Domain ownership references
* Business information displayed on the website

Flag anything that could cause Business Verification issues.

---

## Meta Developer App

Audit every Meta configuration.

Check:

* Facebook Login
* Instagram Graph API
* OAuth redirect URIs
* App Domains
* Bundle IDs
* Package names
* Deep links
* Universal Links
* Client IDs
* App Secret usage
* Redirect logic
* Production vs Development mode

Report every missing or incorrect configuration.

---

## Permission Audit

Identify every Meta permission currently required by ROOK.

For each permission explain:

* Why ROOK needs it
* Which feature uses it
* Whether it requires App Review
* Whether it is optional
* Whether it can be removed

Produce a final list of only the permissions required for launch.

---

## Authentication Audit

Walk through every login flow.

Inspect:

* Facebook Login
* Instagram connection
* Session handling
* Token refresh
* Logout
* Expired token handling
* Error handling

Find every possible failure point.

---

## Reviewer Experience

Pretend you are a Meta reviewer.

Attempt to follow the review flow from launch to connecting a Meta account and using the protected features.

Identify every place where a reviewer could become confused or blocked.

Recommend improvements.

---

## Final Deliverable

Produce a launch readiness report with:

1. Critical blockers
2. High-priority issues
3. Medium-priority issues
4. Low-priority improvements
5. Business Verification readiness score (0–100)
6. Meta App Review readiness score (0–100)
7. Exact action plan to reach 100% readiness before submission.

Do not make code changes automatically unless they are clearly safe. Present findings first with file references and recommended fixes.
