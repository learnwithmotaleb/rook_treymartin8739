Rook is your AI-powered identity bodyguard — actively monitoring the web so impostors can't steal your identity while you sleep.

WHAT ROOK DOES
• Scans Instagram, X (Twitter), TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn, and Snapchat for fake accounts using your name, photos, or bio
• Uses reverse image search powered by Google Vision AI to find your stolen photos posted by impersonators — even on platforms you haven't connected
• Detects fake accounts stealing your captions, username variations, and profile details
• Sends real-time push alerts the moment a threat is found — with full AI-generated evidence and an explanation of why it was flagged
• Provides step-by-step takedown guides and pre-filled report templates for each platform

YOUR IDENTITY FINGERPRINT
Rook learns what makes you, you — your photos, captions, usernames, and writing style — then continuously compares that fingerprint against millions of accounts across all major platforms. Connected accounts are automatically whitelisted so you never get false alerts about your own profiles.

CONNECTED ACCOUNTS
Connect your social accounts once using secure OAuth. Rook ingests your public photos and content, monitors around the clock, and always knows which accounts are yours.
